Who We Are

One and Associates is a real-estate brokerage practice and advisory business led by CEO Richard Vorndran.

Unlike a traditional real-estate brokerage, the firm is not primarily focused on recruiting agents, managing transactions, or operating as a conventional sales organization. Its role is to help brokerage owners and leaders evaluate opportunities, strengthen their businesses, develop strategic relationships, and pursue sustainable growth.

The firm provides brokerage consultation, referral partnerships, and business-development support designed to help real-estate companies improve performance, expand their reach, and make informed leadership decisions.

Services

Practical advisory and relationship-development services for brokerage owners, CEOs, managing brokers, and other senior leaders.

Brokerage Consultation

Strategic guidance to help brokerage leaders evaluate their business model, organizational priorities, market position, and opportunities for improvement.

Referral Partnerships

Support for developing professional referral relationships, identifying collaborative opportunities, and expanding connections within the real-estate industry.

Business Development

Assistance with growth planning, strategic opportunity evaluation, relationship development, and long-term organizational direction.

How We Are Different

One and Associates works alongside brokerage owners and senior leaders rather than competing with their existing brokerage operations.

  • Identify opportunities for growth and improvement
  • Strengthen brokerage strategy and business models
  • Develop referral and partnership relationships
  • Improve organizational effectiveness and market positioning
  • Support confident decisions about expansion and collaboration
